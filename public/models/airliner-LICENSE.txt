Aircraft model: Boeing 777-200 (NASA N577NA "Airborne Science Laboratory")
Source:       NASA Airborne Science Program — "B777 - LaRC"
              https://airbornescience.nasa.gov/3d-models/
              (file: B777_LARC_AIR_1026.glb)

Modifications made for this project:
  - All NASA livery was removed from the base-colour textures (red "NASA"
    wordmarks, meatball logos, blue world-map nose cone, "N577NA"
    registration, "National Aeronautics and Space Administration /
    Airborne Science Laboratory" text). No NASA branding remains in the
    model.
  - Base-colour and metal-roughness textures downscaled 4096 -> 2048 for
    web delivery; the two unused textures were shrunk to 64 px.
  - Node scale 0.01 baked to 1 (positions were already in metres).
  - The German black/red/gold tail + winglet livery is NOT baked into
    this file; it is applied per-fragment at render time by
    src/lib/flight/aircraft.ts (onBeforeCompile), so the stripes stay
    crisp on the fin's sparse tessellation.

Replaces the previous public/models/airliner.glb, which was a low-detail
passenger airliner from Grab3D (https://grab3d.com) licensed CC0.
